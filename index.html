The concept

Same energy as cars & coffee. Just swap the horsepower for hardscape.

Cars-and-coffee meetups work because nobody has to trailer their car anywhere — you just show up. Fish & Coffee borrows that same idea for aquascaping: instead of everyone hauling glass, soil, and livestock across town, the showroom's own floor tanks and Hakkai builds do the showing off. You bring the parts that travel easily — plant trims, dry hardscape, a spare piece of gear — and a cup of coffee gives everyone a reason to actually stand around and talk instead of just scrolling past each other's tank shots online.

Questions

Good to know before you come

Do I need to bring a tank?

No — please don't. The showroom's floor tanks and Hakkai builds are the display for the morning. If you want to bring something, plant trims, dry hardscape, or spare gear are easy to carry and easy to trade.

Is there a cost to attend?

It's free. Coffee is provided, courtesy of a neighboring Convoy restaurant. The coffee partner changes from meetup to meetup, and we'll name them in each event announcement.

Do I need to RSVP?

Not required, but signing up below means you'll get an email with the exact date and location as soon as it's set, plus that month's theme if there is one.

Is this beginner-friendly?

Yes. It's built for every skill level — first-time tank owners are just as welcome as people running full CO2 setups. Nobody's grading your aquascape.

Where exactly is it?

Pet Zone Tropical Fish on Convoy St in San Diego — the same shop that hosts the Hakkai nature-aquarium builds you'll see in the showroom.

Don't miss the next one.

One short email before each meetup — date, location, and that month's theme. No spam, no fish oil ads.
